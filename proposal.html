My Creative Portfolio

1. Website Purpose

The purpose of this website is to present a professional demonstration of my creativity and developing technical abilities. This portfolio will show my range in interest while displaying what I create and skills I develop to potential employers, collaborators and clients.

2. Target Audience

My website is intended for:

  • Potential employers
  • Artists and designers
  • Clients
  • Individuals interested in my artwork

3. Portfolio Components and Research

Research that I gathered shows that my creative portfolio should display a persons accomplishments, skills, and abilities. The Components listed below will be included in my portfolio:

  • About me: Share my creative background, interest, and goals.
  • Projects: Displays selected artwork/ design projects.
  • Skills: Highlights artistic skills and technical abilities.
  • Resume: Showing my education, work experience, and qualifications.
  • Contact: Provides ways for potential clients and employers to reach me.
